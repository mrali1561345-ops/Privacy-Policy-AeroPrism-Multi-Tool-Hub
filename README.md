# Privacy Policy - AeroPrism Multi-Tool Hub

**Last updated:** October 2026

**AeroPrism: Multi-Tool Hub** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates regarding user data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
AeroPrism is an offline-first utility application. We **do not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal information, inputs, or generated data. All calculations and text processing occur locally on your device.

## 2. Data Security
Since the application operates entirely on your device without sending data to external servers, your data remains completely secure on your personal hardware.

## 3. Third-Party Services
AeroPrism does not integrate third-party data tracking, advertising SDKs, or external analytics services that collect personally identifiable information.

## 4. Device Permissions
The application only utilizes standard system clipboard functions when explicitly requested by the user (e.g., tapping "Copy" to save generated passwords or text analysis results).

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
